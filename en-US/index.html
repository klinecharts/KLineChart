Use Cases

KLineChart works best as a charting foundation inside finance products, rather than as a standalone component.

Trading terminals and market views

For professional trading terminals and market views that bring display, analysis, and interaction together. Configure panes, drawing tools, and axes to match your workflow, and plug into realtime or historical feeds with minimal friction.

Finance apps and embedded modules

For finance apps and embedded chart modules where consistency, interaction polish, and bundle size all matter. Zero dependencies, mobile-friendly gestures, and a styling model that fits existing design systems.

Quant research and strategy simulation

For research platforms, backtest panels, and strategy drills that need deeper customization. Extend with custom indicators and overlays, push bars from local data for replay, and build full analysis workflows around your models.

Multi-symbol market dashboards

For trading desks and operations monitoring that need several symbols in view at once. Combine multi-pane layouts, long-range scrolling, and realtime feeds without losing readability on a single screen.
